Behavioural Intelligence

The BS Score —
behavioural trust verified

The INFRAGATE Behavioural Score (BS 0–100) adds a trust dimension that document verification alone cannot provide. It measures how actors actually behave — not just what they claim. Four dimensions. Three data sources. One score.

Dimension 01
Promoter Behaviour
Responsiveness to requests, document consistency over time, disclosed vs actual project status, prior platform history, and HCS-U7 cognitive authentication signals.
Dimension 02
Institutional Behaviour
DFI and bank engagement patterns, mandate adherence, speed to respond, historical deal closure rate, and alignment between stated priorities and actual deployment.
Dimension 03
Contractor Behaviour
Project delivery track record, local content compliance history, dispute resolution pattern, sanctioned entities cross-check, and subcontractor management quality.
Dimension 04
Advisor Behaviour
Conflict of interest flags, quality and consistency of past advisory work, professional sanctions history, independence from project sponsor, and accreditation status.
Three data sources combined: HCS-U7 cognitive authentication signals (via IA-SOLUTION) · Platform interaction and engagement history · Third-party data (credit bureaus, OFAC / EU / UN sanctions lists, public records, court filings, regulatory databases)
